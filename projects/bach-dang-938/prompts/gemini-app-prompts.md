## 00-cast

```
Create a character reference sheet (square image): five stick-figure characters standing side by side in a single row, full body, front three-quarter view, evenly spaced, each with a clearly different expression and pose.
1) NGÔ QUYỀN (hero, Vietnamese general): calm determined face, firm brows, a warm red cloth headband tied at the back with two short tails, a simple red sash across the chest suggesting light armor, holding a long wooden spear upright.
2) DƯƠNG ĐÌNH NGHỆ (older Vietnamese governor): short beard drawn with a few strokes, a small yellow official hat, kind face, hands behind his back.
3) KIỀU CÔNG TIỄN (traitor): thin, shifty narrowed eyes looking sideways, sly crooked smile, no colored accessories, slightly hunched.
4) LƯU HOẰNG THÁO (young Southern Han prince commander): youthful overconfident grin, chin up, a tall deep navy blue official hat, a navy cape, one hand on hip, the other pointing forward.
5) SOUTHERN HAN EMPEROR: a deep navy blue crown with a flat board and short hanging tassels, long navy robe drawn as a simple outline with navy trim, stern face, arms folded. Style: minimal hand-drawn line art on a plain pure white background (no texture, no grid, no shading, no gradient). Fine, slightly irregular black ink pen outlines. Slender stick figures: modest round hollow white head, small black oval eyes, clearly drawn expressive eyebrows and mouth, single black line torso and limbs, small oval hands and feet, about 4.5 heads tall. Only a few flat marker accents in warm red, warm yellow, leaf green and deep navy blue on clothing and props. 10th-century Vietnam. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. Isolated subject with generous white margin. No blood.
```
